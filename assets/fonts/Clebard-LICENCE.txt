Based on TeX Gyre Heros: Copyright 2007-2009 B. Jackowski and J.M. Nowacki
(on behalf of TeX Users Groups), Vietnamese characters added by Han The Thanh,

Clebard is a modified version of TeX Gyre Heros. It is NOT the original
TeX Gyre Heros: as requested by the GUST Font License, the fonts and the
files have been given new names.

Changes made by LaFleurStudio (2026)
------------------------------------
- Width extended to 140 %, vertical stems brought back to their target
  thickness (outlines offset horizontally).
- Six weights obtained by offsetting the outlines of TeX Gyre Heros Regular
  and Bold: Demi (400), Short (600), Tall (800), Grande (1000),
  Venti (1200) and Trenta (1400).
- Horizontal strokes thinned where needed to keep counters open; dots and
  accents moved apart from the letters. In Trenta, the glyphs that would
  close are taken from Venti and thickened horizontally only.
- Spacing and kerning taken from Calebard (LaFleurStudio).
- Embedding flag (fsType) set to 0: the fonts can be installed and embedded
  freely.
- Outlines converted to TrueType; names, vertical metrics and weight
  classes changed.

The scripts used to make these changes are in the source/ folder.
